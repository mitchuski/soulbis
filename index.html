Swordsman protocol

The boundary
belongs to the person.

The Swordsman’s role is to protect: carry your terms, limit disclosure, and guard the actions an agent may take on your behalf. The Mage’s role is to learn and create within those boundaries. You remain the person choosing the terms.

Our aim is for the Swordsman protocols to help form the base of a trusted internet for AI agents and First Persons—the people whose agency they serve. Identity, consent and the scope of a promise should be verifiable, with each person able to choose what to disclose and which actions to authorise. This is the foundation we are building towards.

We are connecting the City Key to the browser extension as an expression of the agent’s VTA: sign in, select the key, and see permitted agent state in the Star. The key carries the evolving record; City services check the scope of each action. Try Star Key →
