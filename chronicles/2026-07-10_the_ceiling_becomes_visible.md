## Verdict

The V6 moving ceiling is now something you can watch. Three rooms carry the
same reading in three idioms: a shell that closes on the star, a flood that
rises through the lattice, a ring that contracts onto the sigil. One model,
R(t) = R₀·e^(g·t) with shelf life t* = ln(1/R₀)/g, identical defaults and
honesty labels across all three. Display only, off by default, page-local.
No geometry, no κ, no key content, no custody was touched.

## What was the star before today

The star tetrahedron manifold rendered a timeless guarantee: 64 vertices,
the 96-edge holographic boundary ∂M, the succ wheel, the white heart at the
core reachable only through both agents. V6's central claim — that the
person's entropy H(X) is fixed while adversarial capacity grows, so every
static guarantee carries a date — existed on the page only as one sentence
in an inspector footer. The geometry said forever; the model says until t*.

## The reading in each room's idiom

- **/star — the shell.** The ceiling is a place the adversary's capacity
  occupies: a translucent veil and slow-turning net outside the lattice
  that descend onto the 96-edge boundary as R(t) → 1. The boundary itself
  breathes faster as t* nears. At breach the shell passes inside, the
  heart pulses coral: exposed.
- **/lattice — the floodline.** The ceiling is a tide over the sovereignty
  strata: every vertex whose stratum fraction (popcount/6) falls below
  R(t) veils violet, bottom-up — stratum 0 floods first, V63 (111111)
  last, all 64 at breach. The story line counts vertices within reach.
- **/sigil — the dated reading.** The sigil is a key's face stamped at a
  date; the ceiling says the reading has a shelf life. A violet ring
  contracts from the canvas edge onto the 64-glyph rune-row as R(t) → 1,
  breathing at the star's tempo curve; past t* it passes inside and the
  constellation dims: the guarantee has expired. The κ derivation received
  zero edits — the conformance vector was re-executed and reproduces
  byte-for-byte (sha256:07f20f68…83d1).

## Postscript — the epistemics pass (same day, after the push)

The First Person asked whether the breach visual makes sense. The audit
found the grammar faithful (the shell-to-boundary gap IS the capacity
deficit; the boundary is fixed because H(X) is fixed; the sigil's expiry is
C82 with no stretch) and two corrections owed, both applied:

- **The breach overstated.** At R ≥ 1 the model says reconstruction is no
  longer information-theoretically impossible — not that it has happened.
  The star and lattice story lines said "what the boundary sealed at year 0
  is readable now"; they now say "nothing in the mathematics prevents
  reading what the boundary sealed at year 0." The sigil already spoke
  correctly ("this sigil's guarantee has expired").
- **The freeze understated.** V6's sharpest point is retroactive: breach
  exposes the past walk, not just the future. The T∫(π) value no longer
  merely freezes at breach — it visibly DRAINS (half-life ~1.5s) while
  R ≥ 1, the accrued reading claimed by the ceiling. Scrub back below t*
  and accrual resumes from what remains.

Known liberty, kept and labeled: the lattice flood's per-stratum ordering
(popcount/6 < R(t)) is an illustrative interpolation — the register's R(t)
is a global ratio, not a per-vertex one.
